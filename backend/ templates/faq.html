How accurate is the premium calculator? ▼
Our calculator uses a trained Random Forest machine learning model that learns from historical insurance data. It provides estimates that are typically accurate within 10-15% of actual quotes. For the most accurate quote, we recommend consulting with our insurance specialists directly.
What factors affect the premium calculation? ▼
The main factors are: Age (younger = lower premium), BMI/Health Status, Smoking status (smokers pay higher), Region (varies by location), Gender (statistical factor), Coverage amount, and Number of dependents.
Can I update my information to see new quotes? ▼
Yes! You can edit any previous calculation from your history. Just click the "Edit" button on any record, update your information, and the premium will be recalculated instantly based on the new data.
Why does smoking increase premiums so much? ▼
Smoking is directly linked to serious health conditions like heart disease, lung cancer, and stroke. Smokers file more insurance claims and face higher medical costs. That's why insurance companies charge 30-50% higher premiums for smokers compared to non-smokers.
How can I lower my premium? ▼
The best ways to lower your premium are:
• Quit smoking (biggest impact)
• Maintain a healthy BMI through exercise and nutrition
• Get regular health checkups to prevent chronic diseases
• Manage stress and mental health
• Consider a higher deductible for lower premiums
Visit our Wellness Hub for detailed tips!
Do age and location really matter that much? ▼
Yes, both significantly impact premiums. Age affects risk profile - older individuals generally face higher premiums. Location matters because healthcare costs, climate, and regional disease prevalence vary. Our analytics show up to 30% premium variation by region and 5-year age groups.
Is my personal information secure? ▼
Yes. Your data is encrypted during transmission (HTTPS) and stored securely in our database. We never share your information with third parties without consent. You can review our full privacy policy in your account settings.
How do I export my calculation history? ▼
Go to your Calculation History page. At the top, you'll find export options for CSV (for spreadsheets) or JSON (for data analysis tools). Select your format and download your complete history with all calculations and justifications.
Can I delete my account? ▼
Yes. Go to Settings > Security and select "Delete Account" in the Danger Zone. This will permanently delete your account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.
How do I change my password? ▼
Go to Settings > Security. Enter your current password, then your new password twice for confirmation. Click "Update Password" to save changes.
