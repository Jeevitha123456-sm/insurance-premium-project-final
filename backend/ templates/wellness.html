🏆 Your Path to Lower Premiums

Discover evidence-based health recommendations that not only improve your wellbeing but can also significantly reduce your insurance costs.

🏃
Exercise Regularly
Physical activity is one of the most powerful ways to improve health and reduce insurance premiums.
  • Aim for 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week
  • Include strength training 2+ days per week
  • Stay consistent with your routine
💰 Save 10-15% on premium
🥗
Healthy Nutrition
What you eat directly impacts your health and longevity. A balanced diet reduces health risks.
  • Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables
  • Choose whole grains over refined carbs
  • Limit processed foods and added sugars
💰 Save 8-12% on premium
😴
Quality Sleep
Poor sleep is linked to numerous health conditions. Getting quality sleep is essential.
  • Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night
  • Maintain a consistent sleep schedule
  • Create a dark, cool sleep environment
💰 Save 5-10% on premium
🚭
Quit Smoking
Smoking is the single most impactful factor on insurance premiums and health outcomes.
  • Use proven cessation methods and support
  • Consult with healthcare providers for guidance
  • Join support groups and communities
💰 Save 30-50% on premium
🧘
Stress Management
Chronic stress increases health risks. Managing stress improves overall wellbeing.
  • Practice meditation or mindfulness daily
  • Take regular breaks from work
  • Engage in hobbies and activities you enjoy
💰 Save 5-8% on premium
🏥
Regular Checkups
Preventative care catches health issues early, reducing long-term medical costs.
  • Schedule annual health screenings
  • Monitor blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Keep vaccinations up to date
💰 Save 3-5% on premium

📊 Impact on Your Premium

Implementing all 6 wellness recommendations can lower your annual premium by 50-60%, potentially saving thousands of dollars.

Timeline

Health Benefits

Your Next Steps
